Meet the Owner

Hi, I'm Reuben Gabriel

Owner and operator of Vista Terrains.

I've spent the last 10 years working hands-on in landscaping, hardscaping, grading, land clearing, and tree removal. Throughout my career, I've had the opportunity to work with experienced crews, operate heavy equipment, and tackle a wide variety of projects. Along the way, I've learned that quality work, attention to detail, and keeping your word are what truly make a difference.

After years of putting my experience to work for other companies, I decided it was time to take the next step and build something of my own. That's how Vista Terrains (VT Land Services) got started.

My goal is simple: show up when I say I will, do the job right, and leave every property better than I found it.

I proudly serve homeowners, builders, and landowners throughout Lincoln, Burke, Catawba, and Caldwell Counties, providing dependable land clearing, grading, tree removal, and property improvement services.

When you choose Vista Terrains, you're working directly with me from start to finish. I take pride in every project, big or small, because my name and reputation stand behind the work.

I look forward to earning your business and helping bring your property goals to life.
